# Themes & Motifs — Jack Campbell

## Core Themes

> What is this author really writing about beneath the plot? What questions or ideas keep appearing across their work?

## Recurring Motifs

> What images, symbols, situations, or phrases recur? What do they seem to represent or reinforce?

## Philosophical / Ideological Underpinning

> What worldview or beliefs seem to shape the author's work? What values do their protagonists tend to embody or struggle with?

## Moral Complexity

> How do they handle moral grey areas? Are their stories clear-cut or ambiguous? Do villains have valid points?

## What the Author Seems to Care About

> Based purely on what they write about repeatedly and passionately — what do you think they're personally invested in?

## How Themes Are Delivered

> Are themes stated explicitly, implied through events, or embedded in character choices? Are they handled with a light or heavy hand?
